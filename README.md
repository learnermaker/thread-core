# thread-core

> A responsibility ledger for people and agents. Model who owns what, detect what a change breaks, hand off with the least context, and resolve on evidence, not on "okay".

**Status:** under construction for the THREAD hackathon entry (Oct 2026). The full README, quickstart and examples arrive in v0.1.0.
